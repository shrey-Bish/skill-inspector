3 · The catch, four beats, plus end states
1 · Calm
Agent and skill working. Gentle drift only. Lasts as long as the run.
2 · The reach · 0.7 s
Arrow leaves the skill toward the wall. Agent and skill freeze.
3 · The stop · 0.9 s
Arrow hits the glass, wall flashes once, arrow falls back and fades.
4 · The proof · holds
Box goes still and dims. Caught-request panel opens beside it (see 03) and stays until the reader moves on.
Clean run, finished
No arrow. The file shows as tidied. Decoy key untouched.
Every run ends here
The box dissolves to an outline. Feed ends with “Test space destroyed”.
